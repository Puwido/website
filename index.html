-- about

Ten years on Roblox.
Five of them writing Luau.

I was a player long before I opened Studio, and I stuck around long enough to start building things myself. Most of what I write now runs on the server: moderation tools, anti-exploit checks, admin panels and data saving, plus the menus and loading screens that sit on top of them.

I try to write code the next person can pick up without guessing: clear names, every request checked on the server, and settings kept in one config file.

Outside Roblox I also write C#, C++, HTML and Java.
